NextNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NextNest ("NextNest", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you connect an eBay account to the NextNest application.

1. Information We May Receive

When you choose to connect your eBay account, eBay may provide NextNest with information that is necessary to provide the features you have authorised. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include information relating to:

  • your eBay seller account;
  • listings and inventory;
  • orders and fulfilment status;
  • shipping and tracking information;
  • seller account policies and marketplace settings;
  • advertising or listing-performance information;
  • buyer or seller communications where specifically authorised; and
  • other eBay information made available through authorised eBay APIs.

NextNest does not ask for, collect, or store your eBay password. Authentication is performed through eBay's authorised sign-in process.

2. How We Use Information

We use information obtained through eBay only as necessary to provide, maintain, secure and improve the NextNest application and the eBay-related features that you have authorised.

This may include:

  • displaying and managing authorised seller information;
  • analysing listings, inventory and marketplace activity;
  • supporting listing, fulfilment, tracking and seller-management workflows;
  • monitoring application reliability and security;
  • maintaining audit records relating to authorised application activity; and
  • complying with legal, regulatory and eBay Developer Program requirements.

Information obtained through eBay is not used to bypass eBay permissions or for purposes that have not been authorised.

3. Artificial Intelligence

NextNest may use artificial-intelligence systems to assist with analysis, classification, drafting, product research and other application functions. Where practicable, personal information is removed, minimised or redacted before information is provided to an AI service.

AI-generated recommendations do not independently authorise financial, marketplace or account actions. Application rules and authorised workflows remain responsible for determining whether an action is permitted.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell eBay user personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are reasonably necessary to operate NextNest, such as cloud hosting, database, security, monitoring or technology providers. Such providers are used only for legitimate application purposes and are expected to protect information appropriately.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, regulation, legal process or an applicable eBay Developer Program requirement.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

Access credentials, API secrets and authentication tokens are treated as confidential and are not intentionally exposed in application logs or public content.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the legitimate purpose for which it was obtained, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We will delete applicable personal information when it is no longer necessary, when a valid deletion request requires us to do so, when eBay requires its deletion, or when our participation in the relevant eBay Developer Program services ends, subject to any lawful retention requirements.

7. eBay Account Closure and Deletion Requests

NextNest is designed to comply with applicable eBay account closure, deletion and marketplace-account-deletion notification requirements.

Where eBay provides a valid account-deletion or account-closure notification, NextNest will process the request in accordance with applicable eBay Developer Program requirements and applicable law.

8. Your Choices

You control whether you authorise NextNest to access your eBay account. You may revoke an application's eBay authorisation through applicable eBay account settings or by contacting us.

You may also contact us regarding access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with NextNest, subject to applicable law and eBay requirements.

9. Development and Sandbox Testing

During development, NextNest may use the eBay Sandbox environment. Sandbox accounts and test information are used for application-development and integration-testing purposes and are not intended to contain real customer personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when NextNest functionality, legal requirements, or eBay Developer Program requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with its updated date.
